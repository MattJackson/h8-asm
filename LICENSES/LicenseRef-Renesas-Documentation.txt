LicenseRef-Renesas-Documentation
================================

This is not a licence grant. It is a statement of what certain files in this
repository are, so that an automated scanner and a human reader both get a
true answer instead of a convenient one.

WHAT IT COVERS

  spec/H8-300-programming-manual.pdf        H8/300 Programming Manual, ADE-602-025 Rev.1.0
  spec/H8-300H-software-manual.pdf          H8/300H Series Software Manual, REJ09B0213-0300
  spec/H8S-2600-2000-software-manual.pdf    H8S/2600, H8S/2000 Series Software Manual, REJ09B0139-0400
  spec/H8SX-software-manual.pdf             H8SX Family Software Manual, REJ09B0102-0400
  spec/H8-compiler-assembler-v7-manual.pdf  H8S, H8/300 Series C/C++ Compiler, Assembler,
                                            Optimizing Linkage Editor User's Manual, REJ10J2039-0100

  spec/*.txt                                plain-text dumps of the above, produced with
                                            pypdf so that the encodings can be grepped

  h8-asm source, tests, examples, documentation and CI configuration, and

WHY THEY ARE HERE

  h8-asm encodes and decodes H8 instructions. Every encoding claim it makes is a
  claim about what these documents say, and a claim like that is only worth
  anything if the reader can check it. They are kept in the tree so that
  checking is a grep rather than a download — see spec/README.md.

  They are reference material for developing and reviewing this crate, not a
  component of it: Cargo.toml's `exclude` keeps spec/ out of the published
  .crate (enforced by the `package` job in .github/workflows/qa.yml).
